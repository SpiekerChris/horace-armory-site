The plan, briefly

People should be able to look at a firearm without being rushed, then stay for a cup and a conversation. That is the whole concept: a local counter, a coffee bar, and a hangout, instead of a big-box aisle.

A Type 07 FFL is a federal firearms manufacturer’s license. The SOT is the tax status that goes with certain NFA work. Those may be part of the shop later, if they are approved. They are not part of it now, and the site is not taking orders.
