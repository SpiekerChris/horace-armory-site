Firearms

What the counter is for.

A neighborhood place to look at a firearm and ask a plain question. Horace Armory is not open, it is not licensed, and this page is not an offer to sell.

Not open. Not licensed. No online sales. Chris Spieker is pursuing a Type 07 FFL/SOT. That is an application, not an approval. There is no cart and no checkout.

Planned categories, not a stock list

When a license is in hand and the doors are open, the plan is a general gun shop. These are the kinds of things that the counter is for. No brands or models are listed, because none are in stock.

  • Rifles, shotguns, and handguns The everyday categories of a small shop.
  • Ammunition For the firearms the shop carries, once it carries any.
  • Accessories Cases, locks, cleaning gear, and similar everyday items. Not a catalog.
  • Transfers In-person transfers of firearms bought elsewhere, only if the license allows it at the time.

Manufacturing and NFA work, later

A Type 07 FFL is the federal license for a firearms manufacturer. An SOT, a Special Occupational Taxpayer, is the tax status that goes with certain NFA work. Chris is pursuing both.

If that license is approved, the firearms side may later include manufacturing and NFA work. It does not include them now. This website will not take deposits, preorders, or build requests.

The law, in plain language

All firearm sales will follow federal, state, and local law. A transfer happens only when the buyer and the firearm meet every requirement that applies. Horace Armory will not ship a firearm from this website. Sales, when they exist, happen in person.
