The hangout

A room worth staying in.

The hangout is the other half of the idea. Not a lobby on the way to the counter. A place to sit, drink the coffee, and talk. The room is still a plan. The shop is not open.

Regulars, once there are any

The hope is a shop where faces repeat. Neighbors. People who already know what they want. People who have questions and would rather ask them sitting down. The point of the chairs is conversation.

None of that exists yet. There is no membership, no reserved seat, and no list of regulars.

Closer to a coffee shop than a superstore

Horace Armory should feel like a local spot: wood, coffee, and time. The firearms are the business. The room is why someone might stay after the business is done. It is meant to be welcoming. It is not meant to feel like a tactical big-box store.

Welcoming does not mean casual about the firearms. When sales begin, they happen in person, and they follow federal, state, and local law.

Reasons to show up

Coffee meetups, range days, and other gatherings will live on the events page once any of them are real. Nothing is scheduled. The events page is a set of samples so you can see the shape of it.
